Privacy Policy — Project Professor

Last updated: 6 November 2026

“Project Professor” (the app) is a private tool used only by the owner of the Project Professor channels to publish the owner's own videos and read the owner's own channel statistics. It is not offered to the public.

What data the app accesses

  • Through the YouTube Data API: permission to upload videos to the owner's YouTube channel and to update their titles, descriptions and privacy status.
  • Through the YouTube Analytics API: read-only statistics of the owner's own videos (views, watch time, likes, comments, shares, subscribers gained).

The app does not access, collect or store personal data of viewers or any other users. It does not read comments' authors, contacts, e-mail, or any other Google account data.

How data is used and stored

Statistics are saved to the owner's private spreadsheet to measure which videos perform well. Access tokens are stored only on the owner's computer and are never shared, sold or transferred to third parties.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The app also uses YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
